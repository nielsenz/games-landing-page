Field manual

THE SMALL PRINT.

Mint bugs are yours. Coral bugs are the rival colony. Food remains yours after your bugs leave, but enemy bugs on a point block its income.

Click / dragSelect friendly bugs
Shift + click / dragAdd to the selection
Right-clickMove and fight; target an enemy to focus it
A, then left-clickThe same command, without a right-click
Space / 1 / 2 / 3Select all / scouts / beetles / spitters
Q / W / ERecruit scout / beetle / spitter
FSugar rush for selected bugs
D / Alt + right-clickRetreat home / move without fighting
R, then left-clickSet the rally point for new recruits
P or EscapePause / resume

Destroy the rival nest to win. At five minutes, the nest with more health wins. Equal health goes to the colony holding more food points; an exact tie is a draw. Cookie drops are bonuses, not victory points.

No fog of war, workers, construction, or tech tree. Normal orders fight enemies along the way. Retreat orders ignore enemies until they reach home. On a touch screen, tap a bug to select it, then tap empty ground to command.
