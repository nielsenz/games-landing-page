YOUR QUARRY

Brambleback.

All moss, bad manners, and a very breakable stone horn. The trick isn’t hitting harder. It’s knowing when.

01 READ THE TELL

The marked ground is the danger zone. Every attack gives you a way out.

02 TAKE THE OPENING

Let the blow miss. Heavy strikes hit harder, but commit you for longer.

03 BREAK THE HORN

Hit the pale horn at the front. Break it for a stagger, a weaker rush, and 16 health.

LEARN THREE TELLS

HORN RUSHWait for the lock. Dodge sideways. Bait a wall crash.
TAIL SWEEPBack and sides are unsafe. The head is the opening.
BODY SLAMThe circle is fixed. Get clear before it lands.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows, J, K, Space.
Mouse: point to aim, hold left to cut, right-click for heavy.
Keyboard and touch attacks face the monster automatically. P pauses. R retries.
