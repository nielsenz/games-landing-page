DREADWORKS / THE HOSTILE ARCHITECT
Build the level. Release the heroes. Regret the gaps.
Survive 12 waves to win. Choose a level to start a fresh run.

Click a shop card, then a tile to place it. To sell during building, choose Sell traps and tap a placed trap for a 60% refund (rounded up). Right-click also sells. Choose a shop card to return to placing. 1–5 select cards, Space releases a wave, P pauses, R restarts after defeat or victory.
Start with 24 gold: try three complementary pieces. Spikes reset in 3s; saws in 0.8s; crushers recover for 3.2s after impact. Springs near gaps can catch survivors.
Blocks delay heroes, but stranded heroes break through after 1.5s. Kills pay bounties; wave-clear bonuses start at 5 gold.
Cannons reload in 2.2s. From wave 9, reinforced balloons take two hits. Layer your air coverage.
From wave 4, armored knights take only ¼ damage from spikes. Use saws, cannons, or crushers to finish them.
