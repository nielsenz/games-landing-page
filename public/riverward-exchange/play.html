Welcome to your riverside shop

Make the right deal.

You have 360 coins, a little warehouse, and fourteen days to turn your stall into a chartered trading house. Reach 900 coins in cash. Or simply keep trading afterward.

01
Read the difference.Riverward is your home quay. Mossmere’s farms are upstream; Emberforge’s ironworks sit beyond the canal locks. Buy and Sell are your actions.
02
Make a small first deal.Nara needs to clear some cloth. Try an offer around 80 coins, then compare buyers. The whole lot—not a single unit—is being negotiated.
03
Let the barges do the moving.Home trades settle now. River and canal cargo arrives in 1–2 days; remote sales pay on arrival. Sale prices are locked when booked; unsold inventory is not.
04
Watch why prices change.Buying drains stock. Selling fills it. Residents consume goods; producers replenish them; one rival chases gaps. Bulk trading changes your own price.

No walking, restocking chores, timers, or punishment for taking your time. Incoming purchases reserve warehouse space. There is no upkeep bill in this prototype.
